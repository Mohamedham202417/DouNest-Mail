Privacy and safety notice

Incoming messages pass through Resend and a temporary copy is stored in DouNest's EU database for up to one hour, then removed by periodic cleanup. The email provider may retain data under its separate policy. Do not use this service for sensitive messages, account recovery, or financial information. Outbound email is disabled, and attachments are not available for download.

A few simple steps

How to use a temporary email address

  1. Create a random address ending in @mail.hmdevelop.me.
  2. Enter it at the service where you expect a one-off message; incoming email only is supported.
  3. Messages appear automatically or after you choose Refresh. Only plain text is shown; HTML is not executed or rendered.
  4. The address and messages expire after about one hour. You can delete them earlier.

Temporary email FAQs

How long does the address and inbox last?

The inbox and its messages expire after about 60 minutes. A periodic cleanup job removes expired records, and you can delete the inbox earlier from the interface.

Is this mailbox private and safe for important accounts?

No. Do not use it for banking, medical accounts, password recovery, or sensitive information. It is for temporary receiving only; Resend and Cloudflare may process data under their separate policies.

Can I send email or download attachments?

No. The service only receives messages. It may show the number of attachments, but does not download their contents; message HTML is not rendered.

Is it free, and is there a creation limit?

The DouNest interface is currently free, with a technical limit of up to 8 new addresses per network address per hour. Availability may also depend on infrastructure-provider limits.

Why has my message not arrived?

Delivery may be delayed or rejected by a sender or email provider; messages may also be filtered or the address may have expired. Delivery is not guaranteed. Refresh the inbox and check the address.
